# Eldoria Wiki — Crafting & Upgrading

## Upgrade tracks
Weapons: +1 to +10. Cost scales: +1–+5 = 200g + 2x Iron Shard per level. +6–+8 = 800g + 4x Steel Bloom + 1x Ember Core. +9–+10 = 2500g + 1x Dragonscale + 1x Moonstone.
Success rates: 100% to +6, 80% at +7, 60% at +8, 40% at +9, 25% at +10. Failure downgrades by 1 (protected with Ward Charm).

## Crafting stations
Forge (weapons), Alchemy Lab (potions), Enchanting Table (affixes). Tier 3 Forge requires Stronghold level 5 + quest "Embers of the Fallen".

## Best early farms
Iron Shard: Goblin Mines floors 1–3. Steel Bloom: Sunken Quarry elite Golems. Ember Core: Cinder Caves boss (guaranteed) or auction house ~1200g.

## Enchants
Weapon enchants (pick 1): Fiery (+12% fire dmg), Keen (+8% crit), Swift (+10% attack speed). Armor enchants stack to 3.
