# Eldoria Wiki — Classes

## Mage
Base HP 320, MP 540. Passive: Spellweave — every 3rd spell costs 30% less mana.
Core stats: INT > Haste > Crit. Best weapons: Runebound Staff (+18% cast speed), Ember Tome (+22% fire dmg).
Subclasses at level 30: Pyromancer (DoT focus), Frostwarden (control), Arcanist (burst).

## Ranger
Base HP 410, MP 220. Passive: Swiftstep — +10% move speed after dodge for 3s.
Core stats: DEX > Crit > Haste. Best weapons: Stormbow, Twin Daggers.
Subclasses: Deadeye (crit), Beastmaster (pet), Trapmaster (AoE control).

## Knight
Base HP 680, MP 120. Passive: Bulwark — blocking reduces damage by 45%, perfect block reflects 50%.
Core stats: STR > DEF > HP. Best weapons: Dawnbreaker Greatsword, Aegis Shield.
Subclasses: Guardian (tank), Berserker (DPS), Paladin (heal/support).

## Respec
Respec costs 500g at level <40, 2000g + 1x Moonstone above. Cooldown 24h.
