# Patch Notes v1.5.0 — "Emberfall" (2026-09-02)

## New content
- New zone: Cinder Caves Depths (recommended level 45+). New boss: Magma Golem, weak to Water, immune to Fire.
- New weapon: Runebound Staff (+18% cast speed, +12% mana regen). Drops from Arcanist's Trial (weekly).

## Combat
- Magic damage formula: MATK coefficient 1.25 → 1.35. MDEF coefficient 0.8 → 0.7 (mages buffed).
- Fire vs Wind bonus 20% → 25%.

## Classes
- Mage: base MP 500 → 540. Spellweave now every 3rd spell (was every 4th).
- Mage / Frostwarden: Ice Wall HP 400 → 600, cooldown 18s → 14s.
- Ranger / Beastmaster: pet revive time 30s → 15s. Knight / Paladin: Lay on Hands heal 30% → 35% max HP.

## Crafting
- +9 success 35% → 40%, +10 success 20% → 25%. Dragonscale now tradable.
- New enchant: Stormcaller (+10% lightning dmg, +5% move speed).

## Balance note
Devs: "Mage underperformed by ~12% DPS in v1.4. Patch 1.5 buffs magic scaling and Pyromancer DoT to close the gap."
