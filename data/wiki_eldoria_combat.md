# Eldoria Wiki — Combat System

## Damage formula
Physical damage = (ATK * 1.2 - DEF * 0.8) * CritMult * ElementMod.
Magic damage = (MATK * 1.35 - MDEF * 0.7) * CritMult * ElementMod.
Crit chance caps at 60%. CritMult is 1.5x base, 2.0x with Deadeye ring.

## Elements
Fire > Wind > Earth > Water > Fire. Fire deals +25% to Wind, -15% to Water.
Light and Dark are neutral to all except each other (+20% both ways).

## Stamina & Dodge
Dodge-roll costs 25 stamina, grants 0.4s i-frames. Perfect dodge (within 0.15s of hit) restores 15 stamina and triggers 1s slow-mo at level 3+ Timing Charm.

## Boss: Storm Wyvern
Weak to Earth. Enrage at 30% HP: attack speed +30%, spawns lightning orbs every 12s. Orbs can be destroyed with Earth spells (2 hits).
